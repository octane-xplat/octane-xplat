### Slow down motion while debugging

Open the settings for a running app in DevTools and choose **Slow** to inspect transitions at quarter speed. Motion and its related delays slow down together. You can also set any rate from above zero through one with the CLI.

```sh
xplat motion set-rate --rate 0.25 --json
```
