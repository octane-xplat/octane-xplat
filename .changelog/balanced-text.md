### Balance the lines of a short title

`BalancedText` evens out the length of wrapped lines in titles and short captions. Give it a limited width and set a heading level when it introduces a section.

```tsx
import { BalancedText } from '@octane-xplat/ui'

<BalancedText level={2} style={{ maxWidth: 280 }} text="Make room for the work that matters" />
```
